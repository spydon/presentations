# Icon

`fire.svg` is the fire emoji of [Noto Emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji),
licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE-noto-emoji`), with its
colors changed to Supabase green. `fire_maskable.svg` is the full bleed variant
with the flame inside the maskable safe zone.

They are the source of the favicon of the landing page, and of `web/favicon.*`
and `web/icons/Icon-*.png` of every presentation. Render them again after
editing them or adding a presentation, with Google Chrome installed:

```sh
melos run icons
```
